## 3. About this game

You wake to a hiss of air inside a damaged cryo-pod. Outside the escape module, sunrise curves around Jivagraha: a tiny living planet you can walk all the way around. Your suit has limited oxygen. Your ship has very few answers.

Start with what you can carry. Explore luminous forests, scan strange life, gather ore and ice, and dig into the crust. Build the systems that keep you breathing, then connect auto-drills, smelters, storage and conveyors into a factory that works while you explore.

Every machine leaves its mark. Smoke hazes the sky. The water and soil change. The forest responds to how you build, and careful choices can help it recover. The Harmony system makes your decisions visible across the whole planet.

Deep below the trees stirs Mooladri, the Root Guardian: a towering shape of roots and mossy bark, crowned with glowing root tips. Its home needs understanding. Trace its roots, move machinery, restore the air and replant the bloom to calm it and open the valley.

SARATHI, your damaged ship AI, offers precise advice and the occasional dry observation. Recover recordings, follow clues about Project Amarnath, and prepare your first launch. Jivagraha is only the beginning. Beyond it lie six more locations, starting with the mineral-rich world Taptashila.

## 4. Features

- Walk the entire curved surface of a tiny spherical world.
- Dig for materials beneath forests, rocks and wreckage.
- Link drills, conveyors, smelters and storage into working factories.
- Keep oxygen and power flowing from sunrise through night.
- Watch Harmony change skies, water, soil and living plants.
- Calm Mooladri by restoring its roots, air and soil.
- Scan wildlife and uncover recordings through the Codex.
- Build towards your first spaceship launch.

## 5. What is in the Chapter 1 demo

- Chapter 1 on Jivagraha only; roughly an hour of guided play.
- Third-person exploration, digging, suit oxygen, crafting and shelter power.
- Research, auto-drills, conveyors and early factory automation.
- Harmony, scanning, the Codex, memory logs and Mooladri's calming puzzle.
- A first lift-off cliffhanger; other worlds are reserved for the full game.
