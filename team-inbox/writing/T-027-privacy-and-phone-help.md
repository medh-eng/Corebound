## Privacy notice

### What we collect

Corebound itself collects nothing about you. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or online features. You do not need to create an account to play.

The game asks for no permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary server logs when your browser loads the game. This is separate from Corebound collecting information about you. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for information about its hosting service.

### What stays on your device

Your saves and settings stay on your own device, in browser storage. They are not sent to the developer. This includes your saved game and the settings you choose while playing.

### Children

The same privacy approach applies to children: Corebound collects nothing from them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or online features for children or other players.

### Changes

This notice describes the current game. If its privacy features change, this notice will need updating.

### Contact

Contact the developer, Medhanshu Shahane, through the game's store page.

## Playing on a phone

- Turn your phone sideways; use your left thumb's joystick to walk.
- Push the joystick to the edge to run.
- Drag elsewhere to look; hold Scan to scan.
- Hold Mine and slide to mine and aim.
- Tap Jump to jump; hold Jump for the jetpack.
- Aim at a machine; tap Use.
- Tap Bag for your backpack; Build and Belt open building tools.
- Tap Codex, Planet, O₂, Pick up or Dance for other actions.
- While building, tap Place, Turn or Cancel.
- Tap ✕ to close panels; tap the gear to pause.
- In Chrome, open ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
