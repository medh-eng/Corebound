## Goal
M4 ends with a free Chapter 1 demo on itch.io and a Steam page for wishlists. Write the words for both pages. The Director fits them to each site's limits and adds the screenshots; the Commander approves the final text.

## Read first
- `AGENTS.md`; `docs/GDD.md` §1 (pitch), §2 (pillars), §10 (Harmony), §12 (Guardians), §13 (worlds), §14 (the first hour), §15 (story: do not reveal the twist).
- `src/data/names.ts` for the names (Jivagraha, Mooladri, SARATHI and so on).

## Write (one Markdown file, these sections in this order)
1. **Short description** (Steam's "short description"): at most 300 characters including spaces. Count them and write the count after it.
2. **Tagline**: 3 options, each under 12 words.
3. **About this game**: 180–250 words in short paragraphs. Open with the wake-up on a tiny round planet; then digging, building and automating; then Harmony (the world answers how you build); then the Root Guardian, calmed and never fought; end on the first lift-off toward the other worlds.
4. **Feature bullets**: 6–8 bullets, each under 15 words.
5. **What is in the demo**: 4–6 bullets, honest about scope (Chapter 1, Jivagraha only, about an hour).
6. **Steam tags**: 12 tags, most important first, chosen only from tags that exist on Steam (for example Survival, Automation, Base Building, Exploration, Cozy, Sci-fi, Open World, Crafting, Atmospheric, Singleplayer, Building, Relaxing, Third Person, 3D, Story Rich, Space, Colorful, Nature). Do not use Violent or Combat tags.
7. **AI-content disclosure (draft)**: Steam asks how AI was used. Draft 80–120 plain words saying: concept art, textures and 3D models were generated with AI image and image-to-3D tools from prompts written for the game, then checked and adjusted; some writing was drafted with AI assistants and edited; music and sound effects come from human artists on Pixabay; there is no live AI-generated content while playing. The Director will correct the details.

## Rules
- **No violence words** anywhere (no fight, kill, battle, weapon, enemy, defeat, conquer). Guardians are **calmed**.
- Do not name or compare to other games, studios or people. Do not promise features outside the GDD.
- Credit line if you mention who made it: "A game by Medhanshu Shahane" only.
- British spelling. Friendly, clear, for players aged 10–25.
