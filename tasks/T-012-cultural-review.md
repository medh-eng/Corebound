## Goal
Corebound's look is "Indo-futurist solarpunk". Check that the prompts the Commander uses for art and music use Indian motifs and instruments accurately and respectfully, and suggest improvements.

## Read first
- `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PROMPTS.md` (§2 style bible, §3–§8 image prompts, §12 music)

## What to make
1. Any prompt wording that could produce something inaccurate or disrespectful (religious imagery, stereotypes, wrong use of a motif), with a better wording.
2. For each world, 3–5 Indian architectural or craft motifs that would suit it (for example jaali, stepwell, chhatri, Warli patterns, bidri inlay), each with one line on where it would appear in the game.
3. For each world's music (§12), the Indian instruments and ragas or scales that fit its mood, and one corrected "style line" per track if you would change it. Remember the music has to loop and layer, so keep to one key per world as written.
4. Anything else in the prompts you would change.
