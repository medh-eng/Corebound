5. **पादकानन:** meaning and pronunciation, and which world or region it suits best (the Szynthars' homeworld in Medhanshu's story).

## Rules
- Easy to say for players worldwide (2–4 syllables preferred).
- No names of gods, goddesses, saints, holy places or religious texts; nothing that could offend.
- Names should sound good together within each world.
