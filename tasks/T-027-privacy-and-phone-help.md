# T-027: Privacy notice and "playing on a phone" help

- **Assigned to:** ChatGPT (chat)
- **Status:** review
- **Priority:** P2 (phone track P3 and M4: needed for the Play Store and the store pages)
- **Deliver to:** `team-inbox/writing/T-027-privacy-and-phone-help.md`

## Goal
Corebound now runs on Android phones (on-screen controls, installable from the browser), and a Play Store version is planned. Stores ask for a privacy notice, and phone players need a short help card. Write both.

## Read first
- `AGENTS.md`; `docs/GDD.md` §16 (the "Phones and tablets" paragraph lists every on-screen control).
- Facts for the privacy notice (check nothing else is claimed): the game has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no online features**; saves and settings stay **on the player's own device** (browser storage); the web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement); the game asks for **no permissions** (no camera, microphone, location or contacts). Contact: "the developer, Medhanshu Shahane, through the game's store page".

## Write (one Markdown file, two sections)
1. **Privacy notice** (150–250 words): plain language for players aged 10 and up, with headings "What we collect", "What stays on your device", "Children", "Changes" and "Contact". Say plainly that nothing is collected. Do not invent laws, company names or email addresses.
2. **Playing on a phone** (8–12 short lines, each under 15 words): how to walk, run, look, mine and aim, jump and use the jetpack, use a machine, open the backpack and the other tools, build (Place, Turn, Cancel), close panels (✕), pause (the gear), and how to add the game to the home screen in Chrome. Use the button names exactly as in the GDD.

## Rules
- British spelling. Friendly and exact. No violence words.
- Credit line if needed: "A game by Medhanshu Shahane" only.

## Done when
- [ ] Both sections, within the word and line limits
- [ ] Every fact in the privacy notice is one of the facts listed above
